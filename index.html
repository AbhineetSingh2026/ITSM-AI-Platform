My Professional Story

I am a strategic and highly resilient IT Service Management (ITSM) professional with over **17 years of experience** leading complex global IT support operations. Throughout my career, I have specialized in **Major Incident Management, Problem Management, and Service Delivery**, managing large-scale critical outages and helping organizations stabilize their environments.

My journey is deeply rooted in modernizing legacy environments. With the advent of advanced AI operations, I have championed the transition from cold, process-driven **SLAs** to human-centered **XLAs (Experience Level Agreements)**, utilizing **ServiceNow** and **Enterprise ESM** platforms to design self-service and auto-triage workflows that remove operational waste.

Currently, I serve as the IT Operations Manager at Global Specialty Insurance Firm (Insurance Operations) in London, and have served as IT Service Desk Manager within Lloyd's Market (Global Lloyd's Syndicate) and Healthcare IT healthcare environments. I hold certifications in **ITIL v3 Foundation, ITIL v4 training, and Project Management Professional (PMP)**.
